# Praxis: Scope Freeze

**Purpose:** Write down exactly what we are building, so we stop re-deciding it every day.
**Source:** Group 4 Project Proposal and Scope of Work, section 1.5. If this page and the proposal ever disagree, raise it with the Product Lead before changing either.

---

## 1. The five stages

1. **Assess:** Learner completes realistic support tasks with an AI assistant.
2. **Map:** We show which skills are below the employer's benchmark.
3. **Train:** Short modules targeted at those gaps.
4. **Verify:** Learner repeats equivalent tasks; work is scored.
5. **Connect:** Employer sees the evidence, if the learner consents.

## 2. Who it's for

Workers and job seekers who need to adapt to AI in their work. The pilot covers one job family, customer support / admin operations, in Kigali, with 10-15 learners and 2-3 employers.

## 3. What we are NOT building

- No job marketplace or job board
- No CV builder
- No mobile app (web application only)
- No second job family
- No automated hiring decisions
- No nationwide rollout
- No proprietary AI model (we use existing AI APIs only)

## 4. Pilot guardrails (from the proposal)

- **Timeline:** 7 weeks (three two-week sprints plus a final evaluation week).
- **Human review:** AI gives training and feedback only. A profile counts as evidence of readiness only after a human has reviewed it.
- **Consent and privacy:** Learners consent before taking part, and again before any profile is shared with an employer. Scenarios use synthetic customer data only.
- **No job promises:** Passing the assessment can lead to a referral, never a guaranteed interview, offer or placement.
- **Success measures:** score improvement from baseline to final, task completion rate, scoring consistency between reviewers, learner usability, and employer judgement of work samples.
