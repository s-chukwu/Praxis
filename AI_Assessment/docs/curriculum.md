## Microcourse contract
Each module requires learning objective; 10–25-minute worked example; safe AI usage demonstration; unsolved practice; feedback; evaluation-ready evidence; accessibility equivalent; version and author; acceptable licensing. Videos are optional; use written interactive labs and human review where possible.

## Course assignment principles
Allocation depends on observed baseline skill gaps, target-track skill requirements and prerequisites. Candidate may manually request any additional course; doing so doesn't change verified competency. Generic 'prompt engineering' should not be compulsory for someone who already demonstrates effective AI verification. Do not train people to deploy unsupervised customer-facing AI without guardrails.
